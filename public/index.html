    Why it matters

    Outages knock out phones, shops and transport at the same time.

    Medicine spoils

    Insulin needs cold storage. We find sites with power.

    No transport

    A driver comes to people who cannot travel.

    Info changes hourly

    Answers come from the live site list.

    Too few coordinators

    The agent handles intake. People approve risks.

    Without Lifeline

    • Long call queues
    • No way to check what is open
    • Paper lists out of date by lunchtime

    With Lifeline

    • An answer in seconds
    • Stock checked live
    • Routes update automatically
